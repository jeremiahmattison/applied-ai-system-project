# Dog care guidelines

## Exercise
- Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of walking or active exercise every day, split into one or two walks.
- High-energy breeds such as border collies and huskies may need 90 minutes or more of daily exercise.
- Puppies need short, frequent play sessions of 5 to 15 minutes rather than long walks.
- Senior dogs benefit from shorter, gentler walks twice a day to protect their joints.

## Feeding
- Adult dogs should be fed twice a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart.
- Puppies under 6 months need three to four small meals a day.
- Fresh water should be available at all times and refreshed daily.

## Grooming
- Brush short-haired dogs weekly and long-haired dogs daily to prevent matting.
- Bathe a dog every 4 to 8 weeks unless it gets dirty sooner.
- Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks, and brush teeth several times a week, ideally daily.

## Health
- Schedule a vet checkup at least once a year, and twice a year for senior dogs.
- Give flea, tick, and heartworm prevention medication monthly.
- Keep vaccinations up to date according to the vet's schedule.

## Enrichment
- Dogs need daily mental stimulation such as training, puzzle feeders, or play for 10 to 15 minutes.
- Social time with the owner or other dogs prevents boredom and anxiety.
