# Cat care guidelines

## Exercise
- Cats benefit from two or more interactive play sessions a day, each lasting 10 to 15 minutes.
- Wand toys and chasing games mimic hunting and help prevent obesity.
- Indoor cats need climbing spaces and scratching posts for daily exercise.

## Feeding
- Adult cats should be fed two meals a day, or measured portions through the day.
- Kittens need three to four small meals a day.
- Fresh water should be available at all times, and bowls washed daily.

## Litter box
- Scoop the litter box at least once a day, and twice if possible.
- Fully replace the litter and wash the box every 1 to 2 weeks.
- Provide one litter box per cat, plus one extra.

## Grooming
- Brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats daily to reduce hairballs.
- Trim nails every 2 to 3 weeks.
- Brush teeth several times a week if the cat tolerates it.

## Health
- Schedule a vet checkup at least once a year, and twice a year for senior cats.
- Give flea prevention monthly and keep vaccinations up to date.
- A sudden change in appetite, litter box habits, or energy is a reason to call the vet.

## Enrichment
- Rotate toys weekly and offer window perches or puzzle feeders to keep cats mentally active.
