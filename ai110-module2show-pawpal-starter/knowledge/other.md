# General pet care guidelines

## Exercise
- Every pet needs daily activity appropriate to its species, such as supervised time outside its cage or enclosure.
- Small mammals like rabbits and guinea pigs need several hours of daily exercise space.

## Feeding
- Feed on a consistent daily schedule, and measure portions to avoid overfeeding.
- Fresh water should be available at all times and refreshed daily.
- Remove uneaten fresh food after a few hours to prevent spoilage.

## Habitat
- Spot-clean the cage, tank, or enclosure daily, and do a full clean every 1 to 2 weeks.
- Keep temperature and lighting appropriate for the species.

## Grooming
- Check nails, coat, and teeth weekly, and groom as the species requires.

## Health
- Schedule a vet checkup at least once a year, preferably with an exotic-pet specialist when needed.
- Watch for changes in appetite, droppings, or activity, and contact the vet if they last more than a day.

## Enrichment
- Offer new toys, hiding spots, or foraging activities each week to prevent boredom.
